## Name

Not This.

## Short description (132 characters max on Chrome)

Collapses low-effort Reddit comments (GIFs, "This.", lol, bare links, bots, copypasta) so you can read the actual discussion.

## Full description

Tired of scrolling past reaction GIFs, "This.", "lol", and bot replies to find the actual
conversation? Not This. collapses low-effort Reddit comments automatically. Each one is
still there, folded up with a small badge saying why, and expands with one click.

WHAT IT COLLAPSES
• Comments that are nothing but a GIF, an image, or emoji
• Stock phrases: "This.", "lol", "Take my upvote", "Username checks out", r/whoosh and hundreds more
• Bare links with no commentary
• Bot replies: AutoModerator, "I am a bot" footers, RemindMe! invocations
• Well-known copypasta
• Optional: quote-only replies, very short comments, downvoted comments, and any users you mute

Every rule has its own switch. Turn on the ones you want.

WHAT IT NEVER DOES
• Collapse a comment with real words in it (unless you enable the short or downvoted rules)
• Re-collapse something you've expanded
• Vote, post, report, or click anything for you
• Send data anywhere. No accounts, no tracking, no network requests.

ALSO
• Works on new Reddit and old.reddit.com
• Keep replies visible under a collapsed comment, if you prefer
• A counter above the thread ("22 low-effort comments collapsed") with a Show them button, the
  same number on the toolbar icon, and an all-time tally
• Open source, unminified, readable in five minutes

Source code: https://github.com/recruiterguy/not-this

## Permission justifications (Chrome privacy tab)

- **storage:** saves the user's rule toggles and settings, and a single all-time counter.
- **Host permission for reddit.com:** the extension reads comment text on Reddit pages to
  decide which comments to collapse, and modifies the page to collapse them. It runs nowhere else.
- **Data collection:** none. The extension makes no network requests.

## Single purpose (Chrome)

Collapses low-effort comments on Reddit comment pages.
